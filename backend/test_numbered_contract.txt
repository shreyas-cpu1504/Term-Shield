SERVICE AGREEMENT

1. PAYMENT

1.1 The Client shall pay the Company within 30 days of receiving an invoice.

1.2 If payment is delayed, the Client shall pay the applicable late fee.

2. TERMINATION

2.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by providing 30 days written notice.

2.2 The Company may terminate this Agreement immediately if the Client materially breaches its obligations.

3. CONFIDENTIALITY

3.1 Each party shall keep confidential information private.

3.2 Confidential information shall not be disclosed to third parties without prior written consent.
